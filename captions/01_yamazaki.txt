Softly floral, sweet and smooth.

The Yamazaki brings together wine-cask malt and Mizunara-cask malt in a single malt Japanese whisky shaped by water, nature and craftsmanship.

Strawberry and cherry lead the aroma. Honey and an expansive sweetness follow with a smooth mouthfeel, before sweet vanilla and cinnamon leave a clean, pleasant finish.

Enjoy it neat, on the rocks or as a highball.

43% ABV · 700 ml
