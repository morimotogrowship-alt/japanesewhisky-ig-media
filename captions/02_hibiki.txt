# Hibiki Japanese Harmony

Harmony, expressed in amber.

Hibiki Japanese Harmony is a blended Japanese whisky shaped by the House of Suntory’s blending craft. Its 24-faceted bottle reflects the seasons of the traditional Japanese calendar.

On the nose: rose, lychee and rosemary, with mature wood and sandalwood. The palate brings honey-like sweetness, candied orange peel and white chocolate, followed by a subtle, tender finish with a hint of Mizunara oak.

It brings together Yamazaki and Hakushu malt whiskies with Chita grain whisky. Try it neat first to explore its delicate aroma, or enjoy it on the rock or as a highball.

Serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.
