A rare harmony touched by sakura cask.

Hibiki Blossom Harmony 2021 is a limited blend that brings sakura-cask whisky into Hibiki’s layered composition. Floral notes reminiscent of sakura and kinmokusei meet acacia honey, French pear and sakura mochi, with a rich yet refreshing finish.

Start neat to explore its delicate aroma, then try it on the rocks or in a highball. These serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.
