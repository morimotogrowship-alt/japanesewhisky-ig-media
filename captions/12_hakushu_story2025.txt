The Hakushu Story of the Distillery 2025 Edition is a limited single malt composed from smoky Hakushu malt matured in bourbon barrels. Its official notes evoke pineapple, vanilla cream and hojicha, followed by soft wasanbon-like sweetness and a finish of ember smoke and gentle bitterness.

Enjoy it slowly neat, on the rocks, or as a forest-style highball. These serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.
