Silky malt, shaped by a distinctive still.

Nikka Coffey Malt is a malt whisky distilled in traditional Coffey stills at the Miyagikyo Distillery. Citrus, pear and vanilla lead into malty sweetness, banana and cinnamon, followed by a clean, gently oaked finish.

Try it neat first, then on the rocks or in a highball. These serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.
