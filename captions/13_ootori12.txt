A quiet meeting of age, malt and Mizunara oak.

OOTORI 12 Years Mizunara Cask Finish is a pure malt whisky made by combining four 12-year-old Scottish malt whiskies with Japanese malt matured for more than 30 years, then finished in new Hokkaido Mizunara oak.

The profile is light and gentle on the nose, delicate and smooth on the palate, with restrained sweetness and a long, quiet finish.

Try it neat first to explore its aroma and oak structure. A few drops of water or a single large ice sphere offer two editorial alternatives.

43% ABV · 700 ml
