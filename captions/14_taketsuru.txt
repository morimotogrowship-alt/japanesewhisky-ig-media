Two distilleries, one remarkably harmonious pure malt.

Taketsuru Pure Malt brings together the robust character of Yoichi and the elegance of Miyagikyo. The official tasting notes open with fresh apple and apricot, followed by toast and vanilla. Banana and navel orange meet layered malt and peat on the palate, before a sweet, slightly bitter finish of dark chocolate, mellow oak and peat.

For a first tasting, try it neat; on the rocks and a highball offer two different perspectives. Serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.

Named for Nikka founder Masataka Taketsuru, this 43% ABV pure malt was named World's Best Blended Malt Whisky at the World Whiskies Awards 2023.
