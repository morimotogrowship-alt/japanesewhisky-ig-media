Coal fire. Coastal soul.

Yoichi Single Malt is a bold, powerful expression shaped by Nikka's traditional direct coal-fired distillation in Hokkaido. Mellow peat and sea salt lead into smoke, orchard fruit, almond and chocolate, followed by a long, gently smoky finish.

Enjoy it neat or on the rocks—both are official recommended serves.
