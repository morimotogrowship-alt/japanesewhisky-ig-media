Subtle, refined, yet remarkably complex.

The Yamazaki 12 Years Old is a single malt Japanese whisky from Japan’s first and oldest malt whisky distillery.

Its aroma layers peach, pineapple and grapefruit with clove, candied orange, vanilla and Mizunara oak. Coconut, cranberry and butter shape the palate, followed by sweet ginger, cinnamon and a long finish.

Enjoy it neat, on the rocks or as a highball.

43% ABV · 700 ml
