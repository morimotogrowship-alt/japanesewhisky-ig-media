An artisan spirit, ten years in the making.

Ichiro's Malt Chichibu 10 Years is a limited Japanese single malt from Chichibu's artisan distillery in Saitama. Released in 2025 at 50.5% ABV, only 4,956 bottles were produced.

Specialist tasting notes describe peach, apricot, pineapple and vanilla, followed by orange peel, redcurrant, white pepper and roasted malt. These notes are editorial summaries, not official producer tasting notes.

Try it neat, then explore a few drops of water. Serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.

Unofficial editorial feature. Please enjoy responsibly.
