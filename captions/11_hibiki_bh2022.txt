Floral harmony, touched by sakura cask.

Hibiki Blossom Harmony 2022 is a limited Japanese blended whisky enhanced with rare sakura-cask whisky. Sakura, kinmokusei and soft wood lead toward acacia honey, French pear and sakura mochi, ending floral, rich and refreshing.

Try it neat before exploring it on the rocks or as a highball. These serving suggestions are editorial, not an official recommendation.
