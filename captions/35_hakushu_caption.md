A forest-born Japanese single malt: fresh, herbal and gently smoky.

Hakushu Distiller’s Reserve opens with peppermint, melon and cucumber. On the palate, yuzu and grapefruit meet lemon thyme, followed by a subtle smoky finish.

Made at Suntory’s Hakushu Distillery in Hokuto, Yamanashi, amid the forests of the Japanese Southern Alps. The cool, humid climate and soft mountain water help shape its crisp, delicate character.

Try it neat, on the rock, or as Suntory’s Forest Highball with one part Hakushu to three or four parts chilled soda.

Please enjoy responsibly.
